How it works

Connect once.

One sign-in. Inbox Agent does the rest in the background and rings your phone when there's a decision to make. Sync runs every 15 minutes by default — set it tighter or looser in settings.

01

Connect.

Sign in once with Google or Microsoft. We read mail only — the OAuth scope is read-only. Tokens are encrypted with pgcrypto; passwords never touch our servers.

02

Read.

Each new email is summarized into a structured task: who, what, by when, how much. 2FA codes and password resets are filtered before the model sees them.

03

Decide.

You see only what needs a decision. Newsletters and receipts stay in your inbox; they never become tasks or alarms.

04

Remind.

Pair your phone with a six-digit code. Tasks ring as alarms titled with the action verb — Cancel, Pay, Confirm, Renew.

What you get

No more missed renewals.

Inbox Agent watches for charges, renewals, refunds, and replies that need you. It skips the rest. You see a short list, not 400 unread emails.

Common questions

Asked
often.

Do you read all my email?+

Only when a sync runs. We look at new messages, decide if you need to act, and move on. Nothing is stored on our servers after that.

Where does my data live?+

Decisions, settings, and history live in the app on your phone. We don't keep a copy on our servers.

Can I use my own AI key?+

Yes. Plug in your own OpenAI or Anthropic key in settings. You pay the AI provider directly.

How often does it check?+

Every 15 minutes by default. You can make it faster, slower, or turn it off entirely.

Will it ever send email?+

No. We don't request the send scope from Gmail or Outlook. The OAuth screen shows read-only. If we ever change that, you'll see a new consent screen — not silently.

Which email providers work today?+

Gmail (Google Workspace and consumer) and Outlook (personal and Microsoft 365). Apple Mail, Fastmail, and Proton via IMAP are queued for the next release.

What does the alarm actually do?+

It rings as a high-priority notification on Android or iOS with the action verb as the title. Tapping it opens the original email and the suggested action. You can snooze for an hour or until tomorrow.
